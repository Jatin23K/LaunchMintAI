Validator Report Structure

When you submit an idea in the Validator tab — this is the exact structure of what you get, in order.

Phase 1 — Appears Immediately (on submit)
01 🏆 Strategic Verdict Instant
Macro Verdict
One bold headline judgment on the idea
Confidence Score
Animated ring chart — 0 to 100%
Data Confidence
High / Medium / Low based on search quality
Veracity Index
How well the data is grounded in live sources
Strategic Pivot Warning
If the idea needs a pivot — shown as a callout
Agent Reasoning Trace
Expandable — shows the AI's thinking steps
02 📊 DS Intelligence Instant · Parallel to main
XGBoost Score
ML-based success probability score for the idea
Monte Carlo
Simulation of 1,000 possible market scenarios
VADER Sentiment
Sentiment analysis of the idea's framing & pitch
03 📈 Market Intel Instant
TAM (Current)
Total Addressable Market today (e.g. $4.2B)
TAM (Forecast)
Projected market size by 2030
CAGR / Growth Rate
Annual growth % of the market
Revenue Strategy
Recommended monetisation model
Market Timing
Label — Too Early / Right Time / Mature + rationale
Growth Chart
Visual bar chart — current vs forecast TAM
04 🥊 Top Competitors Instant
Competitor Cards
Each competitor as a clickable card with name + category
Click → Deep Dive Modal
Opens full competitor breakdown
Inside Each Competitor Modal
Market & Finance
Funding raised, investors, management team
Product Intel
Pricing model, key features, SWOT note
Tech Stack
Infrastructure, build velocity, platform type
Customer Sentiment
Top complaints, trust score, churn drivers
Marketing
Acquisition channel, SEO keywords, social status
Kill Strategy
How YOUR startup beats this specific competitor
05 🎯 MVP Blueprint Instant
Killer Feature
The single most important feature to build first
Build Priority
What to ship in v1 to validate the idea fastest
06 ⚙️ Execution Strategy Instant
⚖️ Legal
🔨 Product
📣 Marketing
💰 Finance
4 Department Tabs
Switch between Legal / Product / Marketing / Finance
Top 5 Priorities
Numbered action items per department
Department-specific actions
Legal: compliance steps. Product: build tasks. Marketing: channels. Finance: funding steps.
07 🕵️ War Room Intel Instant · Corporate Spy Mode
Battlefield Verdict
One-line competitive landscape summary
SWOT — Strengths
Your idea's core competitive advantages
SWOT — Weaknesses
Internal gaps and vulnerabilities
SWOT — Opportunities
Market windows and untapped angles
SWOT — Threats
External risks and competitive threats
Kill Strategies List
How to beat each competitor — per company
Phase 2 — Loads in Background (a few seconds after main report)
08 🚀 Deep Intelligence Background · ~6–8s

3 expandable accordions. Click each to open. Shows a spinner while loading.

💰
Financial Projection
Pricing model · LTV/CAC ratio · Gross margin · Payback period · 3-year revenue table (Year 1/2/3 with revenue, users, burn) · Fundraising round recommendation · Use of funds · Runway months · Verdict
📣
GTM Strategy
North Star Metric · Top acquisition channels (with CAC, timeline, priority) · Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) · #1 Growth Lever · How to get first 100 customers
⚠️
Risk Scanner
Overall Risk Level (Low/Medium/High/Critical) · Risk cards — each with title, severity badge, description, mitigation · Kill Condition (the one scenario that ends the startup)
09 🧠 Extended Intelligence Background · ~5–7s

2 expandable accordions. Click each to open. Shows a spinner while loading.

👤
User Personas
3 detailed personas — each with Name + Age, Role, Pain Point, Goal, Willingness to Pay, Acquisition Channel
🚩
Red Flags
Critical issues with the idea — each with Flag title, Severity (Critical/High/Medium), Explanation, and How to Fix it · Overall verdict
10 🔗 Research Grounding Instant · with main report
Source Links
Clickable citation cards — title + URL of every real data source used
Source Types
Statista, Gartner, GrandView Research, Mordor Intelligence, news articles
Transparency
Shows you exactly what data powered the report
